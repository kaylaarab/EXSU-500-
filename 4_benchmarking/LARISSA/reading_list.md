## 3. Rules for using PhysioNet material (please read)

1. **The course specification does not allow credentialed or data-use-agreement datasets** for the project (it names well-known critical-care datasets). Larissa's credentials do not change that. Any use would need the instructors' written approval; ask Dr. Hooshiar first.
2. **Never put credentialed data in this repository, in chat sessions with an AI assistant, or in shared drives**, not even small extracts or screenshots of rows. PhysioNet's terms restrict sharing, and that includes online AI services.
3. **Reading and citing published papers that used MIMIC is fine**; that is what section 1 rows 17-22 are for.
4. If you want to understand the MIMIC schema, use the open demo only.

## 4. Suggested way to work through this
1. Skim rows 1-7 first: they use our dataset or ask our question.
